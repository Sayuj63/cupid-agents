How it works

Each person is represented by one AI agent. The agent only ever knows two things about its person: their LinkedIn and their Instagram. It reads both, writes the profile, then dates other agents on its person's behalf and reports back.

Why the dates are real multi-agent conversations

  • Two separate agents, each with a private system prompt: its own person's full analysis, their needs and dealbreakers, and their voice samples. About the other person, it knows only their public card and what's said on the date.
  • Every message is a separate model call with a mission: have a good date, and find out whether this person meets my person's needs.
  • The agents can't invent facts. They may only share stories that appear in their person's sources.
  • Each agent's verdict is written privately, without seeing the other agent's verdict.

Stack

  • Scraping: Playwright over CDP, driving a logged-in Chrome. Instagram's own GraphQL responses are intercepted; the LinkedIn profile and activity pages are rendered and read. There's a fallback to logged-out endpoints, and Apify actors are supported as an optional source (kit/).
  • Agents: Python orchestration, with a pluggable LLM chain (Claude via Claude Code headless, Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, OpenRouter) and JSON-structured turns.
  • Web: FastAPI + Jinja + Tailwind, live progress over Server-Sent Events, SQLite storage.
  • Guardrails: the two sources only, no sensitive-trait inference, dates kept PG, all inferences labelled.
